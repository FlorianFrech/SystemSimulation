\chapter*{Abstract}

An abstract should concisely summarize your research by first stating the motivation or problem your work addresses, followed by the specific objectives or research questions you aimed to answer. \newline

Next, briefly describe the key methods or approaches you employed, then present the most significant results or findings obtained. Finally, conclude by highlighting the main conclusions and the broader significance or implications of your research. Ensure the abstract is self-contained, clear, concise (typically 150-300 words), and includes relevant keywords.
