\chapter*{Acknowledgements}

Express your sincere and personal gratitude to everyone who supported you during your Master's thesis, even those not formally involved. Start with your supervisor and, if applicable, your co-supervisor, then mention family and friends for their support and encouragement. Briefly acknowledge specific help, such as proofreading or discussions. Keep it concise and write from the heart. \newline

\textbf{Typical Phrases:}

\textit{Supervisor/Co-supervisor:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item "A special thank you goes to my supervisor, Prof. [Name], for their excellent guidance, valuable advice, and constructive discussions throughout this work."
    \item "I would like to sincerely thank my co-supervisor, Dr. [Name], for their dedicated support and helpful suggestions."
\end{itemize}

\textit{Family and Friends:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item "To my family, thank you from the bottom of my heart for your endless patience, support, and encouragement during this intense period."
    \item "A big thank you to my friends who always motivated and supported me."
    \item "I would like to thank [Name] for proofreading and their helpful comments."
    \item "Thanks to [Name] for the stimulating conversations and mental support."
\end{itemize}

\textit{Further Support:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item "I thank [Person/Institution] for providing [resources/data]."
    \item "Many thanks to [Person] for their technical assistance."
\end{itemize}

\textit{Concluding Remarks:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item "This thesis would not have been possible without your support. Thank you!"
\end{itemize}
