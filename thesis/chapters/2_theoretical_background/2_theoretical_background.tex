\chapter{Theoretical Background}
\label{chap:theoretical_background}

The chapter provides the fundamental theoretical background to follow the used and introduced concepts in \syssimx{}.
Section~\ref{sec:20_notation_and_conventions} establishes the notation and the terminology used throughout the remainder of the thesis.
Section~\ref{sec:21_continuous_time} reviews continuous-time system modeling, covering state-space representations, ordinary and differential-algebraic equations, and the structural property of direct feedthrough that governs instantaneous input-output dependencies.
Section~\ref{sec:22_hybrid_system} extends this view to hybrid systems by introducing discrete events, state re-initialization, and superdense time.
Section~\ref{sec:23_co_simulation} introduces a tool-neutral definition of a simulation unit, the co-simulation scenario, structural analysis of the coupling graph, execution strategies, algebraic-loop resolution, hybrid co-simulation,
and the realization of these concepts under the Functional Mock-up Interface standard.
Section~\ref{sec:24_modeling_approaches} then characterizes the three modeling approaches integrated in the case study.
These are equation-based modeling, musculoskeletal multibody modeling, and continuum-mechanical modeling.
