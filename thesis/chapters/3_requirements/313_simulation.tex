\subsection{Simulation}
\label{sec:req_simulation}

The simulation requirements describe the capabilities needed to execute the coupled system model as a co-simulation.
A key challenge is that the coupled system may contain subsystems with  continuous dynamics, discrete-time controllers, and event-driven transitions that cross subsystem boundaries.
The simulation workflow must handle all of these consistently within one coordinated execution.

Furthermore, different coupling scenarios may require different orchestration strategies, and strongly coupled subsystems may form algebraic loops that require iterative treatment.
The co-simulation algorithm should therefore be independent of the system model and exchangeable by the user.
