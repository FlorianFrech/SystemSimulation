\subsection{General System Modeling}
\label{sec:req_general_modeling}

The general system modeling requirements describe how users shall be able 
to represent subsystems and assemble them into complete system models.
They cover modularity, explicit physical interfaces, system assembly, and 
support for hybrid dynamic behavior.

Table~\ref{tab:req_general_modeling} lists the user requirements together 
with the derived system requirements for this category.

\textbf{UR-01} & \textbf{The overall workflow shall support component-based 
modeling, where each subsystem is represented as an independent, reusable, 
and parameterizable component with explicit input and output interfaces.} \\
\addlinespace[2pt]
SR-01-01 & The framework shall provide a common component abstraction with 
explicit input and output interfaces for representing subsystem models. \\
SR-01-02 & The framework shall support parameterized instantiation of 
components. \\
\midrule

\textbf{UR-02} & \textbf{The overall workflow shall support explicit physical 
units for variables and parameters, including automatic 
unit validation and conversion.} \\
\addlinespace[2pt]
SR-02-01 & The framework shall associate physical units with component 
parameters and interface variables. \\
SR-02-02 & The framework shall validate unit compatibility when connecting 
interface variables or assigning parameter values. \\
SR-02-03 & The framework shall support automatic conversion between 
compatible units where required. \\
\midrule

\textbf{UR-03} & \textbf{The overall workflow shall support the assembly of 
system models by connecting components.} \\
\addlinespace[2pt]
SR-03-01 & The framework shall support the assembly of system models from 
interconnected components. \\
SR-03-02 & The framework shall provide explicit signal and event connections 
between component interfaces. \\
\midrule

\textbf{UR-04} & \textbf{The overall workflow shall support components with 
continuous-time, discrete-time, and hybrid behavior.} \\
\addlinespace[2pt]
SR-04-01 & The framework shall support components with continuous-time, 
discrete-time, and hybrid behavior behind the same component abstraction. \\
SR-04-02 & The framework shall allow hybrid components to expose 
event-related information required for discrete state changes and 
re-initialization. \\
