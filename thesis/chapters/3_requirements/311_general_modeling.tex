\subsection{General System Modeling}
\label{sec:req_general_modeling}

The general system modeling requirements describe how users shall be able
to represent subsystems and assemble them into complete system models.
They cover modularity, explicit physical interfaces, system assembly, and
support for hybrid dynamic behavior.

Table~\ref{tab:req_general_modeling} lists the user requirements together
with the derived system requirements for this category.

\begin{longtable}{
  >{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{1.75cm}
  >{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{13.25cm}}
  \caption{User and system requirements: General system modeling}
  \label{tab:req_general_modeling}                                                     \\
  \toprule
  \textbf{ID}      & \textbf{Requirement}                                              \\
  \midrule
  \endfirsthead
  \caption[]{User and system requirements: General system modeling (continued)}        \\
  \toprule
  \textbf{ID}      & \textbf{Requirement}                                              \\
  \midrule
  \endhead
  \bottomrule
  \endfoot

  \reqidbf{UR-01}  & \textbf{The overall workflow shall support subsystem-based
    modeling, where each subsystem is represented by an independent, reusable,
  and parameterizable simulation unit with explicit input and output interfaces.}      \\
  \addlinespace[2pt]
  \reqid{SR-01-01} & The framework shall provide a common component abstraction with
  explicit input and output ports.                                                     \\
  \reqid{SR-01-02} & \strut The framework shall support parameterized instantiation of
  components.                                                                          \\
  \midrule

  \reqidbf{UR-02}  & \textbf{The overall workflow shall support explicit physical
    units for variables and parameters, including automatic
  unit validation and conversion.}                                                     \\
  \addlinespace[2pt]
  \reqid{SR-02-01} & The framework shall associate physical units with component
  parameters and ports.                                                                \\
  \reqid{SR-02-02} & The framework shall validate unit compatibility when connecting
  ports or assigning parameter values.                                                 \\
  \reqid{SR-02-03} & The framework shall support automatic conversion between
  compatible units where required.                                                     \\
  \midrule

  \reqidbf{UR-03}  & \textbf{The overall workflow shall support the assembly of
  system models by connecting subsystem models.}                                       \\
  \addlinespace[2pt]
  \reqid{SR-03-01} & The framework shall support the assembly of system models from
  interconnected components.                                                           \\
  \reqid{SR-03-02} & The framework shall provide explicit signal and event connections
  between component ports.                                                             \\
  \midrule

  \reqidbf{UR-04}  & \textbf{The overall workflow shall support simulation units with
  continuous-time, discrete-time, and hybrid behavior.}                                \\
  \addlinespace[2pt]
  \reqid{SR-04-01} & The framework shall support components with continuous-time,
  discrete-time, and hybrid behavior behind the same component abstraction.            \\
  \reqid{SR-04-02} & The framework shall allow hybrid components to expose
  event-related information required for discrete state changes and
  re-initialization.                                                                   \\
\end{longtable}
