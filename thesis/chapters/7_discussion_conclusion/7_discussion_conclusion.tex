\chapter{Discussion and Conclusion}
\label{chap:discussion_conclusion}

This chapter synthesizes the implementation and case-study results with respect to the research questions stated in Chapter~\ref{chap:intro}.
It closes the loop to the introduction by explaining how the evidence supports the thesis contribution.

The discussion interprets the evidence at thesis level.
It distinguishes numerical verification against monolithic OpenModelica references, workflow validation of the combined framework features, and runtime benchmarking.
The chapter then states the limits of this evidence, gives the final conclusion, and derives future work from the remaining limitations.
